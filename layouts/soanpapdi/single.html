The SOAN PAPDI FPGA

*No MCU. Pure FPGA!

Soan Papdi is a learning-focused FPGA board built around the Lattice iCE40UP5K, making FPGA development simple and approachable for learners.

The design is intentional - Just the FPGA, Flash memory, a USB-C port for power and loading circuits, and Basics Input/Output.

Specification

{{/* Left side content */}}
{{/* Right side content */}}
  1. FPGA: Lattice iCE40UP5K FPGA
  2. Logic Resources: 5,280 LUTs
  3. Embedded Memory: 120 Kbit Embedded Block RAM
  4. SPRAM: 1 Mbit (128 KB) Single-Port SPRAM
  5. Clocking: On-chip PLL
  6. Hard IP: 2 × SPI, 2 × I²C
  7. Internal Oscillators: 10 kHz and 48 MHz
  8. DSP Resources: 8 DSP multiplier blocks.
  9. CPU: Capable of hosting RISC-V soft-core CPUs.
  10. Onboard Storage: 128 Mbit onboard Flash memory.
  11. User Interface:
    1. 8 × 3.0 mm LEDs (through-hole)
    2. 4 × SMD LEDs
    3. 8 x DIP switches
    4. 2 push buttons (Programming & Reset)
    5. 10 x I/O pins for ext. sensors & peripherals.
  12. USB: USB-C fully controlled by FPGA (no ext. MCU)
  13. Programming: ⁠Programmable via preloaded DFU bootloader.
  14. Toolchain Support: Open-source toolchains including Yosys, nextpnr, IceStorm, Icarus Verilog, and Amaranth HDL.
